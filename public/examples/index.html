The client delays the approval and the team waitsTranslated from Ukrainian

Work with the part of the logic that is already approved

Step-by-step development and delivery is a key idea in agile methods like Scrum or Agile. I recommend talking to the team about splitting the website into modules or sections, and building and approving them one by one. This lets the design team work well without waiting for the approval of the whole project.

Give the team what is already approved

The team can also prepare drafts for the next steps. They must understand that the logic and the functions will still change. If they have already done everything that is approved, they can simply rest and do nothing.

The question of paying for idle time

If there is a question about paying for their work and for "idle time", discuss it with the customer. Are they ready to pay the agreed sum even if the designers do nothing, or make mockups that will have to be redone anyway? The delay is on the client's side, so they may well agree to this.

Remind the client about the delay

It is important to remind them that the delay is on their side. By waiting, they waste not only their own time. They also bring uncertainty and low motivation into the team.

How to manage User Stories and subtasks in a teamTranslated from Ukrainian

Hi.

This is a great situation. It causes arguments in almost every company)) It does not cause them only where people have already agreed on a way.

My experience shows that some companies use subtasks and some do not. Some people split epics into stories, and some just create tasks.

It depends on many things. There is no universal solution that fits all companies and teams.

In your case, if you use a User Story as a container for related tasks, then:

  • The tasks linked to this story should be split small enough that one developer can do each of them (and it takes no more than 2 days).

  • The statuses of a User Story can be very simple: To Do, In Progress, Blocked, Done/Cancelled. As soon as any task in the story is started, the story should also become In Progress. When all tasks are done, you can change the status of the story too.

  • Who changes the statuses? The interested person is responsible for the status of a story. In your case, this is you))) The developers, testers and designers are responsible for the statuses of the linked tasks. This is the most convenient way.

When several developers work on one task at the same time, this is not normal. When several people work on different tasks in one story, this is fine)

Budget is part of a project manager's responsibilityTranslated from Ukrainian

In fact, managing the budget is usually a natural part of a project manager's responsibility.

A manager often calculates the whole project budget together with the risks and other buffers. Finance is part of resource planning, which the manager leads.

In the Ukrainian reality, few managers are trusted with finances (nobody trusts them with money). So the result is unfair: there is almost no real power to manage, but there is responsibility)

As an experienced project manager, you must understand financial risks, cost price, margin and dependencies. You must also include extra costs, such as sick leave and vacations.

If you lead a PMO, you already keep the picture of the whole company portfolio in your head. Then, besides the budget of each project, you need to think about money moving between projects, and about "donor" projects when needed (some projects can live at the expense of others).

At the delivery level, you work not only with the resources that the project needs. You also take into account business needs, product financial metrics and KPI, value, ROI and so on, when you build business units and plan budgets.

Tables in Excel are the minimum level of involvement in budget management.

Mock interview: job search in the international marketTranslated from Ukrainian

1. Communication style

As far as I know Americans, they have a different temperament and a different understanding of what communication is and how it should happen.
It is important to have a smile on your face. Even a fake one. It is important that you smile. It is important to be polite and to give compliments (we see these as compliments, but for them it is just a normal part of communication).
If you do not smile and do not show friendliness and openness, they see it as a sign of hostility. It will be hard for them to work with you, because for them you are aggressive and unfriendly.
The calm and confidence that you show are great for the Ukrainian market, but not for the American one...

2. Sincerity, readiness to accept your imperfection, admit your mistakes and say what you do not know

We were raised in a way that you must not admit your "problems", because people will not accept you as such a "defective" person.
For them it is not like this. They are ready to support, help, teach, and forgive some mistakes and imperfection. But a person must also admit this and not hide it from others.
It will be uncomfortable for them to work with you, because for them you are too closed. I do not know if there are recordings of real interviews with American companies in open access, but if there are, watch how they go. It may help.
In my opinion, it looks a bit clownish, and you have to get used to it. But for them, our usual behavior is difficult and unpleasant.

3. Theoretical base

I heard that you practise "intuitive Lean management". That is cool. But this Lean must be based on something. To make intuitive decisions, you also need knowledge and experience. If you do not know the documents, you cannot judge their use or value. If you do not know the processes, you cannot evaluate them and make a decision. If you have no experience in risk management and change management, you cannot make important decisions with responsibility, and you cannot facilitate a team well or help them.

4. Business first

The American market is, first of all, business. Second, people. Third, processes.
If the business says that they have Scrum without a Product Owner, then they have Scrum without a Product Owner. And first of all you must understand that this is not the Scrum you know, and that you will have to either adapt to the demands of the business or look for another company.
If the Scrum Master is seen as a team manager, then no matter how you turn it, you will have to be a manager too.

5. Value for the company

Behind every question at an interview there is money. A person hired by a business must either increase profit or reduce costs (in principle, this is also increasing profit).
For every question, try to add an argument for how it has a positive effect on the company's profit.
This money-oriented approach will be appreciated in any company, American or Ukrainian.

6. Before you ask a question in return, ask whether it is appropriate

You asked a lot of questions. That is nice, but at some point I started to feel that we were using the time of our meeting ineffectively. I especially do not advise getting into arguments (yes, you said that it was the only time, but after our meeting I am not sure that is true). People have reasons to say the things they say, to call things by the names they use, and to ask what they ask. You can clarify what is meant, or keep your clarifying questions for the end of the interview, when they give you a turn (sometimes they offer to ask questions right during the interview, but you must be careful with this). Your questions say about you no less than your answers. You also need to take this into account.

You should not do this...
Take time to think (you can think aloud if you feel sure of yourself). And when you understand that you are ready to give a clear and understandable answer, say so. Self-doubt, feedback from the team, and moving to another companyTranslated from Ukrainian

If you worry that you know and can do nothing, do two things:

  1. Check your job description and compare it with the area of responsibility that the company gave you. If there is no clear job description and no clear requirements, ask for them, because without them there will be trouble!

  2. Collect feedback from your team about your work. Run one-to-one or group retrospectives with them about your work. Ask what they expect from you and what you can do better.

About the market and moving to another company

The situation on the market is really hard now. It is not as easy to find a job as it was 2-3 years ago. But if you clearly see that this company does not suit you, it is worth starting to prepare for a move now. Try not to let it affect the quality of your work in the current company. It would be great to get good recommendations from your last company, instead of leaving with a scandal.

About impostor syndrome

It is probably better to fight impostor syndrome with the help of a psychologist. But you can also manage without it.

All people are unique. You cannot become the same manager as another person. You can only be yourself, and this is your value.

Of course, it is good if you understand the principles and basics of management, and have a plan and some clarity, at least at the level you have now. After that, everything depends only on you: on your flexibility, your ability to learn and to negotiate. And your ability to build relationships with people matters a lot too.

The main thing

Your task is to make the project work well. The company should make a profit and everybody should be satisfied. But how exactly you do it is not written in any book. There is only general advice about what to pay attention to. And everyone does it in their own way.
