Who it is for

  • Project and delivery managers who keep getting interviews but not offers.

Not for: a guaranteed offer, a ready-made script to memorise, or someone to write your CV for you.

What you leave with

  • A list of the answers that are weakest, and why.
  • A clearer way to tell the story of your last project.
  • Detailed written point-by-point feedback within 1 working week after the mock interview.

How the hour goes

  1. 10 minutes: the role you are aiming at and your story so far.
  2. 30 minutes: a mock interview with real PM questions, including questions about decisions, conflicts and what you actually did on each project phase.
  3. 20 minutes: honest feedback and what to rework first.

Before the call

Send your CV and the job description you are aiming at, if you have them. If not, a few lines about the role are enough.
