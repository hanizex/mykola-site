Privacy

Visiting the site

The site's own statistics set no cookies. It counts visits with simple first-party events (page viewed, button clicked, scrolled halfway). To count unique visitors per day without tracking anyone across days, the server mixes your IP address and browser details with a secret that changes daily and keeps only a short irreversible code. Your IP address is not stored. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, no events are sent. The same rule applies to Cloudflare Web Analytics, which adds aggregate, cookie-free statistics (country, referrer, device, page speed) and is not loaded at all in that case. Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga) and sends usage data (pages viewed, source, device, approximate location) to Google, which may process it outside the EU. Visitors from the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked first, and nothing from Google is loaded before they accept; for visitors from other countries it loads without a question. Anyone can turn it off at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control count as a refusal.

Requests, intro calls and the quiz

If you request a consultation, the form sends your name, your email or Telegram username, your LinkedIn link (if you give one) and your message to me as a Telegram notification so I can reply. I keep it for up to 12 months, then delete it. Booking a free intro call opens Calendly in a new tab: Calendly collects your name, email and message under its own privacy policy and may set its own cookies there. Payments happen on Monobank or Revolut pages; I never see your card details. The Challenge AI quiz runs in your browser: your answers are not sent or stored, only the fact that a quiz was started or finished is counted like other page events. The demo tracker in the Tools section keeps what you type in your browser's local storage on your own device; none of it is sent to me or to any server, and you can clear it with the reset button or by clearing site data.

The online tracker (free test)

If you use the free online tracker, your personal data is handled in your own AI chat, on your side. No sensitive information, such as your CV, your Fact Bank or your personal history, is passed to the online tracker. It keeps only what it needs to work for you: your search goals (job titles, places, languages, search terms), the jobs it found for you, their statuses, your notes, and the cover messages and other texts you choose to save on a job card. Your personal link is your key and there is no sign-in or cookie: whoever has the link can open your tracker, so keep it to yourself. You can replace the link or delete everything in the tracker settings at any time, and then your goals, cards and notes are removed. Technically, the tracker runs on Cloudflare, and the daily scan of job boards runs as a scheduled job on GitHub. I look at overall numbers and technical errors to keep it healthy, and I do not use your cards for anything else. If you stop using it for a while, scanning for you simply pauses until you come back.
