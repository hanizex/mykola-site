Privacy

Visiting the site

This site sets no cookies. It counts visits with simple first-party events (page viewed, button clicked, scrolled halfway). To count unique visitors per day without tracking anyone across days, the server mixes your IP address and browser details with a secret that changes daily and keeps only a short irreversible code. Your IP address is not stored. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, no events are sent.

Requests, intro calls and the quiz

If you request a consultation, the form sends your name, LinkedIn link and message to me as a Telegram notification so I can reply. I keep it for up to 12 months, then delete it. Booking a free intro call opens Calendly in a new tab: Calendly collects your name, email and message under its own privacy policy and may set its own cookies there. Payments happen on Monobank or Revolut pages; I never see your card details. The Challenge AI quiz runs in your browser: your answers are not sent or stored, only the fact that a quiz was started or finished is counted like other page events.

How long and who to ask

Requests and order records are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted. To ask about or delete your data, contact me at hanizex@gmail.com.
