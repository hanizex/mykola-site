Privacy

Visiting the site

By default this site sets no cookies. It counts visits with simple first-party events (page viewed, button clicked, scrolled halfway). To count unique visitors per day without tracking anyone across days, the server mixes your IP address and browser details with a secret that changes daily and keeps only a short irreversible code. Your IP address is not stored. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, no events are sent. The same rule applies to Cloudflare Web Analytics, which adds aggregate, cookie-free statistics (country, referrer, device, page speed) and is not loaded at all in that case. If you accept analytics in the banner, Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga) and sends usage data (pages viewed, source, device, approximate location) to Google, which may process it outside the EU. Nothing from Google is loaded before you accept. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control count as a refusal.

Requests, intro calls and the quiz

If you request a consultation, the form sends your name, your email or Telegram username, your LinkedIn link (if you give one) and your message to me as a Telegram notification so I can reply. I keep it for up to 12 months, then delete it. Booking a free intro call opens Calendly in a new tab: Calendly collects your name, email and message under its own privacy policy and may set its own cookies there. Payments happen on Monobank or Revolut pages; I never see your card details. The Challenge AI quiz runs in your browser: your answers are not sent or stored, only the fact that a quiz was started or finished is counted like other page events. The demo tracker and your own tracker in the Tools section keep what you type in your browser's local storage on your own device; none of it is sent to me or to any server, and you can clear it with the reset button or by clearing site data.

How long and who to ask

Requests are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted. To ask about or delete your data, contact me at hanizex@gmail.com.
