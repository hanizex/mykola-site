Privacy

Visiting the site

This site sets no cookies. It counts visits with simple first-party events (page viewed, button clicked, scrolled halfway). To count unique visitors per day without tracking anyone across days, the server mixes your IP address and browser details with a secret that changes daily and keeps only a short irreversible code. Your IP address is not stored. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, no events are sent.

Booking a consultation

When you pay and book, I store the payment method you chose, the transaction ID you entered, and the steps you completed, so I can confirm your order. Choosing a time uses Calendly, which collects your name and email under its own privacy policy and may set cookies on that page only. I receive a Telegram notification for each order.

How long and who to ask

Order records are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted. To ask about or delete your data, contact me at hanizex@gmail.com.
