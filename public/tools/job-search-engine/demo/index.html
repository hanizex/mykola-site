JSE demo tracker

This is how the tracker works. It does three jobs: it takes you to the job post by a direct link, it holds the status of every job, and it takes you to the right people on LinkedIn. Jobs are not added here by hand. They come from the scan, and each card already holds the full description.

Invented data: the companies, roles and the person "Alex Example" do not exist. The job link opens an invented sample ad, and LinkedIn searches show unrelated results. In your real tracker these links open the real job post and the real people. Your changes are saved only in this browser. Nothing is sent to me or to any server.

How the tracker and your AI chat work together

The tracker does not write your CV. Your AI chat does. Each does what it is good at.

TrackerHolds the jobs the scan found, each with the full description and the screening verdict. Statuses, links, LinkedIn searches and checkboxes.
Your AI chat (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini)Checks blockers, asks questions, and builds the tailored CV, the cover message and the LinkedIn messages from your Fact Bank and Master CV.
Other sitesThe employer's form and LinkedIn. You apply and send the messages yourself.
  1. Press Copy for AI. The full description is already on the card.
  2. Paste it into your AI chat.
  3. Confirm the findings or correct them.
  4. Copy the CV, cover message and LinkedIn messages from the chat.
  5. Apply, set the status, write to 1-2 people, tick the box.
Today

You move the job into the chat and the results back by copy and paste. Two clicks and two pastes per job.

Free test access

Connect your AI chat to the online tracker through MCP (Claude, ChatGPT). You say "Process the next job from the tracker". The AI reads the card, does the work and updates the card itself. No copy and paste.
