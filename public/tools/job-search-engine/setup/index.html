Build your Fact Bank

One place with everything true about you, each fact with its evidence.

Everything that contains true statements about your work can become facts. The AI reads it, proposes facts with evidence, and asks about gaps. You confirm. The Fact Bank never stops growing: when a new fact shows up in any later chat, the AI offers to add it.

YouBring any source and confirm
⇄
AIExtracts facts, asks, flags conflicts

Sources that work

  • Current CV
  • LinkedIn profile export
  • Answers to questions
  • Extra documents
  • Interview transcripts

Where it lives: Your Fact Bank is a file that lives with you: in your AI project (a Claude Project, a ChatGPT Project or a Gemini Gem), in a folder or drive, or in your own repository. You and your AI choose what works best. You can open it, edit it and take it with you at any time. The tracker never needs it.

Set your preferences

Tell the engine what a good job means for you, with no gaps left.

The AI interviews you topic by topic: location and work mode, roles, seniority, languages, forms of cooperation, pay floor, excluded places and employers, and deal-breakers. Then it checks the whole set for gaps and contradictions and shows a dry-run preview, so you see what the engine would keep and skip before it runs for real.

YouAnswer one topic at a time
⇄
AIInterviews you and checks for gaps

Where it lives: Your preferences are a file you own too. The scan needs only the search rules (job titles, locations, blockers). Your name, work history and pay details do not have to leave your side.

4

Run, apply, track

Get jobs in, decide, apply with a tailored package, and track every step.

Two ways in: scan your company list and job boards every morning, or paste a job ad yourself. Both go through the same screening. The scan stores the full job description on every card, so there is no need to open the ad and copy it. For each job you pick, the AI maps the ad to your facts, checks the ATS gate, and drafts the CV, the cover message and the LinkedIn messages. You review and send. Nothing is ever sent for you.

Where it lives: The tracker holds jobs, statuses, dates, links and short notes. Your CVs and messages stay in your chat and your files. Storing the cover and LinkedIn messages on the card is optional.

Two ways to connect your AI chat to the tracker

The tracker does not write your CV. Your AI chat does. How they exchange data is the only thing that changes.

TodayCopy and paste

Press Copy for AI on a card and paste it into your chat. The chat shows the findings and builds the package. You copy the CV, cover message and messages back into the card and set the status. Two clicks and two pastes per job. Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and other chats.

Free test accessDirect connection (MCP)

You connect your AI chat to the online tracker once. Then you say "Process the next job from the tracker". The AI reads the card with the full description, does the work with you and updates the card itself. No copy and paste. Works with Claude and ChatGPT.

Common mistakes and how to fix them

Think ahead. Most problems fall into the same few patterns. Filter by step.

Fact BankMy facts are vague and have no numbers

Why it happens Without a number or a clear outcome, a fact cannot support a requirement, and the ATS gate will stop.

How to fix it Answer the AI's follow-ups: how many, how long, what changed, who saw it. If no number exists, describe what changed. Never invent a number: a made-up figure fails at the first interview.

Fact BankMy CV and LinkedIn disagree (dates, titles)

Why it happens Recruiters compare both. A mismatch looks careless or worse.

How to fix it The AI flags every conflict. You choose the correct version once, and the Fact Bank keeps it. Then fix the wrong source so the mismatch is gone everywhere.

Fact BankI shared a transcript with other people's names or company secrets

Why it happens You may have a duty of confidentiality, and the AI does not need other people's data to extract your facts.

How to fix it Remove names, client details and confidential figures before sharing. Ask the AI to extract only what you did and achieved, and leave confidential numbers out of the Bank.

Fact BankI am not sure a fact is true or I cannot prove it

Why it happens A fact you cannot stand behind in an interview is a risk, not an asset.

How to fix it Reject it, or edit it to what you can prove. Only confirmed facts are used for CVs and messages.

Master CVThe AI added a skill I do not have

Why it happens Keyword stuffing raises the match score and lowers your credibility.

How to fix it Check that every bullet shows its fact tag (F12, F07). Remove any line without one. The method never closes a gap by adding a keyword with no fact behind it.

Master CVThe CV is too long or too generic

Why it happens Too long gets skimmed. Too generic does not match any ad well.

How to fix it Keep to 2 pages. If you search for different kinds of roles, keep one Master CV per role family and choose the right one for each job.

Master CVI used a fancy template and the ATS reads garbage

Why it happens Columns, tables, text boxes, headers and photos break many parsers.

How to fix it Use the plain structure: one column, standard headings, dates as MM/YYYY – MM/YYYY, simple bullets, .docx. Put contacts in the body, not in a page header.

PreferencesThe digest is empty

Why it happens The filters are too narrow: titles, locations, or too many blockers set to wall.

How to fix it Run the dry run and read the skip reasons. Loosen one rule at a time and preview again. Turn some wall rules into ask so you still see the job.

PreferencesThe digest is full of noise

Why it happens Title keywords are too broad, or the location rules let everything in.

How to fix it Add off-scope words to the exclusion list (for example intern, junior, marketing). Keep P3 hidden. Narrow the title list to what you would really apply to.

PreferencesA good job was skipped as a "wall"

Why it happens A rule is stricter than you meant.

How to fix it Every skip carries a reason. Read it, find the rule, and either relax it or change it from wall to ask. Then run the preview to confirm.

PreferencesI said "remote" but got jobs I cannot take

Why it happens "Remote" is often limited to certain countries or needs a local entity or a visa.

How to fix it Tell the AI where you can legally work. It adds the matching blockers and priorities (P1 to P3), and asks recruiters early about entity and visa when a role may need them.

ScanningI found a job myself. Where do I add it in the tracker?

Why it happens The tracker has no "add job" button on purpose. It lists jobs that came through the scan and screening.

How to fix it Paste the job description into your AI chat to get the screening, CV and messages. To also track the job, ask the AI for a one-job import file and load it with the Import button. A direct connection (MCP) that lets the AI create the card itself is planned.

ScanningI stopped getting new jobs and see a red notice

Why it happens You have 20 or more cards in "Not applied". The tracker stops adding new cards until you process the existing ones.

How to fix it Apply to the jobs you want, and set the rest to "Not a fit". When fewer than 20 wait, new jobs appear again at the next scan.

ScanningA card has no full description

Why it happens Some sources do not expose the ad text, or the page could not be read. This is rare. The card then says so.

How to fix it Open the job link, copy the ad text and paste it into your AI chat yourself. Do not let the AI guess the requirements from the title.

ScanningA company shows no jobs

Why it happens Its career system is not supported, or the address changed.

How to fix it Use the discovery step to find which system the company uses and to validate its endpoint. If it is not supported, paste its job ads yourself. The rest of the chain works the same.

ScanningThe same job appears twice

Why it happens It is posted on several boards or in several countries.

How to fix it The engine matches by link, job id, and company with a similar role, and collapses one posting per country into one row. Different roles are never merged. If a true duplicate slips through, mark one as Not a fit.

ScanningThe digest email does not arrive

Why it happens Mail settings are missing or wrong, or the message went to spam.

How to fix it Use an app password, not your normal password. Send a test email from the last report. Check spam. The scan itself still writes the report even when email fails.

ApplyingThe gate stops: "match below target"

Why it happens Real facts do not cover enough of the requirements.

How to fix it Read the named gaps. If you have a true fact the Bank is missing, add it and run again. If not, skip the job or apply knowing the gap. Never add a keyword you cannot back up.

ApplyingI applied with the same CV everywhere

Why it happens A Master CV works, but an adjusted CV matches the ad better and shows effort.

How to fix it For jobs you really want, run the full step 4 and use the tailored package. Use the Master CV as it is only when speed matters more than fit.

ApplyingI applied twice to the same company within a month

Why it happens A second application close to the first usually gets fewer replies.

How to fix it The full tracker warns you and shows the earlier application. Apply again only if the new role fits clearly better, or write to the recruiter you already talked to.

ApplyingI applied and heard nothing

Why it happens Applications through job sites rarely get replies on their own.

How to fix it Within 3 days of applying, find 1-2 people at the company (recruiter, hiring manager) and send the drafted message. After about a month of silence, send one short follow-up.

ApplyingMy cover message is too long

Why it happens Many forms cut text at 1000 characters.

How to fix it The counter turns red above 1000. Ask the AI to shorten it. Keep five parts: greeting, why you want to join, what you bring, an invitation, closing and name.
