Your browser tracker

Load the jobs the engine found, work through them and keep every status in one place. Your data stays in this browser. Nothing is sent to me or to any server.

Back up with Export. Clearing your browser data deletes this tracker.

Prefer not to run the engine yourself? The online tracker collects fitting jobs for you every morning. Open the online tracker →

How to use it

  1. Run the engine and load out/import_latest.json with Import. Each card brings the full job description and the screening verdict.
  2. Press Copy for AI on a job and paste it into your assistant project.
  3. Paste the assistant's answer into Apply AI result. The tracker reads its card-update block and fills the card.
  4. Apply on the employer's site, set the status, and write to 1-2 people on LinkedIn within 3 days.
