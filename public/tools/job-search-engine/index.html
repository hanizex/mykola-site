Job Search Engine (JSE)

A job search run like a delivery project: automate the repetitive parts, keep a human for the decisions, and measure whether each source is worth keeping.

Try the demo → Setup guide

JSE is my own tool. It is tuned to my search, and the demo uses invented data. The method works for any role and market: change the rules and the company list.

What it does

Two parts. The first runs by itself every morning. The second is a short routine you do with an AI assistant for each job you decide to apply to.

Every morning, by itself

  1. CollectReads open positions from company career systems (public endpoints of ten ATS types) and a few job boards.
  2. FilterKeeps titles that fit and gives every job a location priority, P1 to P3.
  3. Screen the adReads the ad text for hidden blockers: residency, required languages, mandatory certificates. Verdict: wall, ask or ok.
  4. De-duplicateMatches by link, job id, and company plus similar role, against your tracker.
  5. ReportWrites a digest and emails only what you have not seen. Reads employer replies from the inbox, read-only.
↓ You read the digest and pick the jobs. This is the human step.

For each job you pick, with an AI assistant

  1. Evidence mapEach requirement in the ad is matched to a real fact from your Fact Bank. The ad shows what the employer wants, never what you did.
  2. ATS gateThe keyword match must reach your target (mine is 81%). If real facts cannot reach it, the process stops and names the gaps.
  3. BuildA tailored CV, a cover message of up to 1000 characters and LinkedIn messages of up to 500.
  4. Apply and reach outAfter applying, write to 1-2 people at the company within 3 days.
  5. TrackThe tracker moves the card through statuses and shows what to do next.

The CV chain and the gate

The gate is what keeps the tool honest. A keyword is never added without a fact behind it.

Location priority

Every kept job gets a priority from your own rules. These are mine as an example.

  • P1Home city on-site or hybrid, or fully remote
  • P2Relocation within Europe
  • P3Needs a work visa. Hidden by default

Design decisions

APIs firstWhere a company exposes a public jobs endpoint, I use it. Boards are read slowly, the same pages a person sees.
Rules, not AI, for screeningFilters and ad screening are plain rules. Every skip carries a reason, so a wrong skip is easy to find and fix.
AI only where it writesCollecting, filtering, screening and deduplication cost nothing in AI. The assistant is used for the CV, the cover and the messages.
Read-only and safeEmail access never moves, deletes or marks messages. A failing source never stops the daily run. No credentials in code.
A human decidesThe tool proposes. You choose which jobs to apply to.

What stays with each person, and what is shared

Stays with each person

  • Fact Bank: your real facts and evidence
  • Master CV per role family
  • Your company list and rules
  • Job history, statuses and employer replies
  • Email access for the digest and replies
  • The CVs and letters you generate

Shared, free for personal use

  • Fetchers for ten ATS types
  • Filters, priorities and the ad-screening engine
  • Deduplication and the daily digest
  • Tests that run offline
  • The tracker and its data model
  • The CV method and message formats

Build your own

Use the demo to see the tracker, then follow the step-by-step setup guide: it covers the Fact Bank, the Master CV, your preferences and your first run.

  1. Open the demo and click through the cards, statuses and messages.
  2. Download the engine and run the offline demo with an example profile.
  3. Create a project in your AI chat and add the assistant pack. It interviews you and writes your profile, Fact Bank and Master CV.
  4. Run the engine with your profile and load its results into your own tracker, which lives in your browser.
  5. For each job press Copy for AI, paste it into your project and apply the answer.
  6. Apply on the employer's site, then write to 1-2 people at the company.
