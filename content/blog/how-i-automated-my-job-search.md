Someone asked me how anyone can spend 10-14 hours a day on a job search.

I know 28 job sites and 14 Telegram channels. The search alone takes anywhere from 2 minutes to 2 hours.
Most jobs need an updated CV that highlights the key skills: 5 to 30 minutes each.
Then you read the ad, the company page and the reviews. You talk to recruiters. You keep a document with progress, statuses and comments: 20 minutes to an hour.

Then your job search can drag on for months.

## A job search is a delivery project

As a manager, I deal with risks and look for the most efficient way to reach the goal.
So I treat a job search as a project.
Most of those hours are routine. And routine can be automated.

Job Search Engine (JSE) is my own tool for exactly that.
A job search run like a delivery project: automate the repetitive parts, keep a human for the decisions, and measure whether each source is worth keeping.

## What JSE does every morning on its own

Why every morning? On Djinni and DOU, whoever applies first gets the interview invite first.
If you are not among the first 5-10, your chances are close to zero.

The tool has two parts. The first runs by itself every morning.
The second is a short routine with an AI assistant for each job you decide to apply to.

```diagram
type: steps
title: From job collection to application
caption: Steps 1-5 run on their own every morning. From step 6, you decide.
- Collect | Company career systems (ten ATS types) and a few job boards
- Filter | Titles that fit, plus a location priority from P1 to P3
- Screen the ad | Hidden blockers: residency, required languages, certificates. Verdict: wall, ask or ok
- De-duplicate | By link, job id, and company plus similar role
- Digest | Only what you have not seen yet
- Your pick | You read the digest and choose the jobs
- Evidence map and ATS gate | Each requirement is matched to a fact from your Fact Bank
- Build | CV, cover message and LinkedIn messages
- Apply and reach out | You apply yourself and write to 1-2 people at the company
```

Every job gets a priority from your own rules. Here are mine as an example:
→ P1: home city on-site or hybrid, or fully remote.
→ P2: relocation within Europe.
→ P3: needs a work visa. Hidden by default.

Filters and ad screening are plain rules, not AI.
Every skip carries a reason, so a wrong skip is easy to find and fix.
AI is used only where something has to be written: the CV, the cover and the messages.

A human decides. The tool proposes. You choose where to apply.

## The CV: no keyword without a fact

It all starts with the Fact Bank: your real facts and the evidence for them. Plus a Master CV for each role family.

For every job you pick, the assistant builds an evidence map: each requirement in the ad is matched to a real fact from your Fact Bank.
The ad shows what the employer wants, never what you did.

Then comes the ATS gate. The keyword match must reach the optimal level.
If your real facts cannot get it there, the process stops and names the gaps.

The gate is what keeps the tool honest. A keyword is never added without a fact behind it.
Keyword stuffing raises the match score and lowers your credibility.
And a made-up figure fails at the first interview.

Once the gate is passed, the assistant builds a tailored CV, a cover message of up to 1000 characters and LinkedIn messages of up to 500.
You apply yourself. Nothing is ever sent for you.

## The step most people skip

Applications through job sites rarely get replies on their own.
So after applying, write to 1-2 people at the company within 3 days.

Warm contacts and referrals noticeably raise your chances of reaching the technical interview.
The tracker gives you ready LinkedIn searches: 1st circle, recruiter, head or CEO. And it reminds you to write.

Two good applications beat eight lazy ones.

## The online tracker: a free test with your AI chat

Don't want to install anything? There is an [online tracker](/tools/job-search-engine/hosted/).

Every morning it collects jobs that fit your goals and keeps them on your personal page with their statuses.
You connect your AI chat (Claude or ChatGPT) once, and the chat works through the next job with you: it checks the ad against your own facts and prepares the CV, the cover message and the messages.

I just say "Process the next job from the tracker".
Other chats, such as Gemini, work with copy and paste: the tracker has "Copy for AI" and "Apply AI result" buttons for that.

```diagram
type: cards
title: What the tracker shows
mark: check
- Jobs for your goals | Every morning, on your personal page
- Full ad and verdict | The ad text and the result of the blocker screening
- Status and next step | From "Not applied" to "Offer"
- Cover and messages | If you choose to store them on the card
- The LinkedIn step | Ready searches and a reminder to write to 1-2 people
```

The tracker keeps only your search goals, jobs, statuses and notes.
Your CV, Fact Bank and personal history stay in your AI chat.

For now the online tracker reads DOU and Djinni and keeps up to 20 open jobs per person.
This is temporary: more job boards and company career sites are coming.

Access is free during the test. To get your personal link, write to me.
