Your CV says "AI-driven". Your last project says "copy-paste into ChatGPT".

My analysis of IT project manager job postings produced eight groups of skills.
This updated checklist adds English and practical work with AI tools and AI agents.

## 1. Organisation and resource management

Turn scope, people and resources into a workable plan. Make responsibilities clear and keep the work moving.

## 2. Communication and stakeholder management

Listen, explain, present. Find out what stakeholders actually need.
A room full of nodding heads can still contain five different versions of the same agreement.

## 3. Problem solving and decision making

Analyse the facts, question assumptions, find the cause and make a decision.
Scheduling another meeting can wait.

## 4. Leadership and negotiation

Organise the team's work, keep people engaged and negotiate when interests collide.
"Everyone, please work harder" gets old quickly.

## 5. Risk management

Spot potential problems, assess probability and impact, agree on a response.
"We knew this could happen" is an expensive thing to say after doing nothing.

## 6. Time management and prioritisation

Prioritise, delegate and build realistic schedules.
If everything is urgent, someone has avoided making a decision.

## 7. Budget management and cost estimation

Estimate costs, track spending and understand what changes will cost.
Someone will pay for every "small adjustment". Know who, and how much.

## 8. Project management methods and tools

Understand methods, frameworks, tools and the processes from planning to closure.
Be able to explain why you're using them on this project.

## 9. English for IT project management

Documentation, professional literature, terminology, conversations with international stakeholders.

"Advanced English, a must" takes one line in a vacancy.
Developing it takes considerably longer.

## 10. AI tools, AI agents and workflow automation

Knowing what an AI agent is gets you through the introduction.
You also need to implement AI tools and agents in real project workflows.

For example:
Meeting notes → draft tasks → checks for missing owners → status update for approval.

Choose the tools, connect the relevant data and configure the workflow.
Set permissions and human approval points.
Test the output, handle failures and measure improvements in speed or quality.

"I took a course on AI agents" is theory.
Show me what you've implemented.

If your entire AI practice is rewriting emails, there's plenty left to learn.

## How to assess your project management skills

Rate yourself from 1 to 5 in each area.
For every score, recall a real situation that backs it up.

"I read about risk management" and "I managed a project risk" deserve different scores.
So do "I know AI tools" and "I implemented a workflow the team actually uses".

Pick the two weakest areas that matter for the role you want.
Now you have something specific to work on.
