Why go to a paid course when everyone already has ChatGPT?

## Knowledge is no longer scarce

Explanations, examples and templates are easy to find now.
Knowledge on its own has stopped being worth much.

```diagram
type: groups
title: What lost its value and what gained it
# Easy to find
- Explanations
- Examples
- Templates
# Valuable
- Knowing how to apply it
- Knowing where to apply it
- Knowing when to apply it
```

## The blind spot you can't see

Right now I teach a project management course.
And I see how much it matters to review decisions, to get support and to get an explanation at the right moment.

You have your own experience and access to knowledge. But that won't help you see what sits in your "blind spot".

How will you know you're missing [a skill](/blog/it-project-manager-skills-from-job-postings/) if that "something" never even crosses your mind?

## What a teacher sees

During the course we go through the material methodically, topic by topic. And I can clearly see:

```diagram
type: cards
mark: arrow
title: Topic by topic: what a teacher sees
- Speed up | Where we can move a little faster
- Stop | Where we need to stop and go deeper
- Push | Where I need to push to break through resistance
```

That is exactly what makes a course with a teacher so valuable.

## What AI won't do

Because [AI](/blog/why-hire-a-project-manager-if-you-have-ai/) won't tell you about things you don't ask about and that aren't in your context.

AI won't push through your boundaries or break your resistance to everything new.

AI will hand you generic practices for a spherical company in a vacuum.

And in complex, unique situations it will simply start hallucinating...
