"If they fear you, they respect you" – the motto of company leaders on the verge of extinction.

## What fear gives a team and what it costs

Fear in a team gives you a short burst of discipline, or it paralyses the work completely.
But the bill for lost initiative, creativity and decision speed is one you'll definitely be paying off for a very long time!

People stop bringing you uncomfortable truths, because the price of the truth is higher than the price of silence.

```diagram
type: steps
title: How fear turns into a bill
- The truth costs more than silence
- People stop bringing uncomfortable truths
- A culture of "green statuses" and hidden mistakes | Fixing it is extremely hard
- More often the company falls apart than copes with it
```

## What fear does to the system

Fear doesn't hit one spot. It hits five at once.

```diagram
type: cards
mark: cross
title: Five effects of fear in a team
- It blocks growth | People pick safe tasks instead of the ones that are needed.
- It kills initiative | Better to keep quiet than risk being punished.
- It strangles decision quality | The point is not to do it well, but to do it the way the top says.
- It destroys trust | The leader hears from the team only what they want to hear.
- It wipes out respect | The team "obeys" until a crisis hits.
```

## Why ruling by fear no longer works

Authority has been demystified.
The new generation won't put up with manipulation and threats.
Mental health has become a top priority.

People spot a leader's weak points and red flags faster than ever.
The new financial reality creates demand for partnership, not dictatorship.

## My approach: leadership without fear

My approach to leadership rests on something else:

- **Authority through personal example.** I show what it means to be responsible and effective.
- **Trust and shared values.** I know what, besides money, motivates each person on my team.
- **Openness.** I admit my mistakes and apologise sincerely when I've screwed up.
- **Balance.** I have a wife, kids and hobbies. My colleagues see a real person in me.

I use a range of management tools that help the team achieve the best results, not prepare me [a report "I'll like"](/blog/why-one-way-reporting-kills-projects/).

## A strong leader doesn't make people afraid

A strong leader doesn't make people afraid.
They set clear rules, [take responsibility](/blog/project-manager-seniority-is-responsibility/) and give people a caring space to reach their goals.

```diagram
- Brings excuses | Brings solutions
```
