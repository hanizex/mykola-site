If it weren't for them, we could hand our whole job over to AI. Stakeholders are saving the PM profession from automation once again.

## What I've already handed over to AI

I'm happy to use AI to automate the routine.

```diagram
type: groups
title: Who does what
# AI agents
- Preparing reports
- Analysis and summaries
- Document drafts
- Checklists and brainstorming
# Me
- Check
- Edit
- Sign off on the result
```

AI agents have been doing this and a lot more for a long time.

## If that were all a PM did

If that were all [a project manager's job](/blog/why-hire-a-project-manager-if-you-have-ai/) came down to, we'd have been fired long ago. AI does this part almost flawlessly.

## Everything changes once you work with people

```diagram
type: groups
title: Working directly with people
# Someone...
- forgets to warn you that the plans changed
- demands what was never part of the agreement
- demands an instant change of priorities in their favour
- is slow or makes a mistake and won't admit it
- criticises everything in sight, while someone else agrees with everything
- takes on more than they can handle and burns out completely
- on the contrary, won't lift a finger until reminded
# Sometimes you have to...
- show initiative and be proactive
- pay attention and read between the lines
- just agree
- say a firm "nope!"
```

## Where AI is helpless

In every one of these situations AI is helpless.

However much context you give it, it won't replace live, empathetic communication.

## Which PMs are safe for now

So for now we're safe.

At least those of us who keep building their [soft skills and communication skills](/blog/it-project-manager-skills-from-job-postings/).

If your stakeholders have already played out every scenario in the textbook and AI still can't give you good advice on the problem, write to me. I'll share my experience, or we can work through your situation in a consultation.
