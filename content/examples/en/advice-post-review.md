**Repetition**

The word "burnout" is used LITERALLY in every paragraph (I checked). This is too much. Use synonyms, other words, comparisons and other forms.

**The topic keeps changing**

The post starts with one idea, moves to another, and ends with a third. There is no warning and no reason. It is just because it is your post and you do what you want in it.

**Captain Obvious**

You write about obvious things and add nothing new, so there is nothing valuable. Grass is green, the sky is blue. If you hit yourself, it will hurt.

There is no lived experience and no recommendations. I mean, no REAL recommendations. The advice "you must pay attention to this" is complete nonsense.

The last sentence should be a strong ending, a call to action. Instead, it says nothing. It is hard to even understand it, because it starts with "And" and does not continue the previous thought. It looks like it was thrown in from another context.

**P.S. Spacing and formatting**

Your format is not comfortable to read. Use more space and more paragraphs. Much more!

*[…]*

**What to do to add value**

Give examples of concrete situations and ideas, or your own experience: how you can act, what options there are, what the result was, whether there were failures, and what you learned from them. This is where the real value is.

**An honest overall impression**

Your article is NOT bad. It is rather "nothing special". I did not find anything valuable in it.

The style and formatting do not matter much. The repetition does not matter much either.

But the fact that there is nothing of yours in it is the real problem.

If I want to read about burnout without recommendations, I can find a lot of neutral information on the internet. If you want to show yourself and your expertise, this is exactly what you need to do.

> Not "burnout often happens", but "I notice more and more signs of tiredness and apathy".

> Not "you need to be careful and not push the team to the limit", but "I recommend that my colleagues take extra days off when I see the situation getting worse. To reduce harm to the project, I plan a safety time buffer, so my colleagues have time to recover their strength"... Or something like that.
