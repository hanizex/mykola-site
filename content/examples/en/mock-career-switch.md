### 1. Soft skills

Your soft skills are well developed. You can listen to a person, you can create an atmosphere of trust, and you care about kind and gentle relationships. It is especially nice that you are working on developing your emotional intelligence. This is really important when you work with people. And a manager's work is very closely connected with it.

### 2. Hard skills

Your technical skills are quite good for a beginner level, but it is worth paying attention to developing in this direction. Right now they are quite chaotic and not organized. It is clear that you learn everything on the go, and thanks to your strong intelligence you manage it. But it would be good if your approach to learning and development were more organized and goal-oriented.

### 3. Body language, gestures, non-verbal signals and tone of voice

You are a very emotional person. Your emotions burst out like a fountain, often over the edge. From the non-verbal signals it was clear that you were worried and nervous. You calmed down more or less when I reminded you that this was not a real interview but a practice one, but this calm did not last long. In fact, you behave quite naturally, openly and sincerely, but because of nervousness there is a feeling of uncertainty and superficiality. To reduce anxiety and stress, I recommend reading the most common interview questions and preparing answers to them. Here is an interesting collection: https://dou.ua/lenta/articles/interview-questions-project-manager/

### 4. Clarity of thinking and wording of answers

Your answers were, in general, adequate and your line of thought was in the right direction, but the answers came out chaotic and full of "water" and extra information. Several times I had to stop you and make a summary of everything you said, turning it into a specific and short answer. There are 2 types of questions. The first type checks your knowledge and practical experience, and the answer should be short and clear, without extra explanations. The second type checks how you think and solve problems. Here you can think out loud and name the options, with the possible advantages and disadvantages of your ideas. In any case, you should not go too far from the point of the question, because it can create the impression that you cannot answer, or that you even forget what you were asked. I recommend recording your answers to questions on camera, and then watching and listening to them, so it is easier to analyze what can be improved

### 5. Tools and methods

You show quite good knowledge of the main project management tools and an understanding of how and why they are used. You also have an intuitively correct understanding of how to use methods and approaches. This is one of your strengths. I advise you to mention more often, in your answers, which tools and approaches you use to do tasks and solve problems.

### 6. Theoretical knowledge

There is trouble here. This is your weak side. Without theory, you will not go far on practice and intuition alone, so I strongly recommend that you read specialized literature about methodologies and frameworks. Even better, take project management courses. This will help you put everything in order, call things by their proper names (and not "well, that case when we do this thing"), and it will also help you understand better what you already do according to project management principles and what is worth working on.

### 7. Motivation and goal orientation

Another weak spot. What motivates you, interests you and encourages you to develop covers a wide range of possible fields and, unfortunately, does not meet in one specific role. I would advise you to talk to a career consultant, who can help you decide on your profile and build a development strategy to reach your goal. In my view, you could become quite a good project manager, but it may also turn out that this is not the direction in which you would really like to have a career

### 8. Leadership and management

You can lead people, you can define a goal and build a path to it, and you can take responsibility and show initiative. You go boldly into the unknown and you are ready to fight uncertainty, whatever it is connected with. You take care of yourself, the people around you and their well-being and comfort. This is cool! )) These qualities will really help you reach any career goals.
