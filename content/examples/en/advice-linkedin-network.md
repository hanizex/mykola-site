Hi.

**First, you need a filled-in profile.** In general, yours is filled in, but quite poorly. There are many ways to improve it.

**Second, you need to grow your network of contacts.** Add other managers and recruiters to your contacts (I recommend adding 15 people every day). It is also worth liking and commenting on interesting posts.

**Third, you need to post something on professional topics from time to time.** Or repost the posts that you like.

Then, of course, you need to talk to your community. Make a post saying that you are looking for a job, and give the necessary information: what job you are looking for, and what skills and experience you have. This makes it easier to find a match for you.

Personally, I made several posts about my job search. Each of them got 10-20 thousand views, with many reposts and comments. After these posts, recruiters contacted me with different offers.
