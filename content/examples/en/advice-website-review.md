What I noticed when I looked through the site:

- All links open in new tabs. This is not comfortable.
- Every section has a different design and style. Each time you are not sure if you are still on the same site or if you were sent to another one.
- The colours of the text, links and background are hard to read in some places.
- And there are some "defects" in the layout.

I understand that you are not a designer or a layout developer, and you may not care about this. But if you had web development projects, you probably know what clients think is important and comfortable)
