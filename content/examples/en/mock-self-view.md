### 1. Soft skills

From the first seconds of the conversation you fill the meeting with an atmosphere of kindness, safety and trust. You can listen without interrupting, and you can finish your thought when someone interrupts you. You are open to criticism and objections, and you do not react to provocations. You stay calm on the outside, even when you are nervous.

### 2. Hard skills

It is clear that you know well the tools you have used in practice at work, but you have used only a few tools. Your understanding of what a project manager should do is not clear, and because of this you do not concentrate on building project management skills. I can see that you are unfocused and try to take on all tasks at once.

### 3. Theoretical knowledge

You lack theoretical knowledge. It is good that you started to study the basics and prepare for CAPM. As I said during the meeting, it will be very useful if you do not only read to answer on the exam, but also compare what you read with how it works in your practice and ask yourself: "Why is it not like this for us?", "Why is this important?", "Where else can this be used?", "What does this word mean in my work?" and so on.

### 4. Practical experience

For now, your practical experience is very limited. You said this at the beginning of the meeting, and it was confirmed during the interview. The good news is that you can quickly absorb new practice. I can see it from how confidently you spoke about the topics where you had experience. The bad news is that you often try to apply your previous experience to a new situation where a different approach is needed. When you change projects, I recommend asking about the practices used in the new place, or the practices your managers, colleagues and team recommend from their previous experience. Some details and nuances can turn out to be very useful and make the work much more effective and pleasant

### 5. Intuition and creativity

You have a good imagination, great intuition and a good ability to create and imagine. This will definitely help you with risks and with process optimization. It also helps you, with high probability, to give answers right on target, or close to it. Still, there is a risk that you do not have all the information. Ask more clarifying questions, this will give you an extra advantage.

### 6. Body language, gestures, facial expressions, tone of voice

Your skill in staying confident, calm and balanced is impressive! Only sometimes, for a short moment, I could notice signs of doubt, sadness, embarrassment or confusion. But even in those moments you behave naturally and in a lively way, which smooths the effect and makes it even less visible.

### 7. Clarity of speech

Your thoughts are well structured, and your answers are clear and short. You can be brief when you need to be brief, and you can pass on the main point even when the topic is abstract. Your language is clean, and you switch between languages in a really cool way. The only filler word that shows your uncertainty or nervousness is "well" ("ну"), or "weeeell....". I will say it honestly: this is very close to the ideal that I can imagine in the real world, and even during an interview))

### 8. Impostor syndrome

You show real adaptability and flexibility, but it is still clear that you have impostor syndrome, which shows itself from time to time. Maybe it is connected with the fact that you tend to be self-critical, you like to bring everything to a perfect state, and if something does not work out, you reduce the value of your achievements. In fact, if I sum up only for project management, you simply need to learn a bit more theory and practice on additional cases to fix the material in your mind. Make yourself a project called "Getting the CAPM certification". Split it into phases, work on the documentation, describe the processes and risks, define the main deliverables, and so on. Then take some other project and take it apart into pieces)) You can also play with ChatGPT. It can generate tasks for you, check your line of thinking and correct your actions. Everything will be OK.
