### 1. Soft skills

Your soft skills are very well developed. You listen carefully, ask politely, suggest with respect, and you are sincerely surprised and interested. I am sure that it is very pleasant and comfortable to talk to you. You react to stressful situations quite sharply, but you also quickly get your inner balance back.

### 2. Hard skills

You are well organized, you can plan, you keep focus on what is important to you, and you have many other skills that are useful for a manager. I enjoyed watching how you take notes and structure your answers. I missed some clarifying questions from you, but I hope you will learn this over time... Because without it, it will be hard)

### 3. Body language, gestures, non-verbal signals and voice

Your whole body said that you were nervous and not sure about yourself and your abilities. You have a charming smile, and you hide behind it very well. You keep eye contact as much as it is possible online, and overall you manage to stay open, even in such an uncomfortable situation. There were a few moments when you felt confident. It was visible right away, because you became relaxed, calm and strong. Develop this, it will help you many more times in life.

### 4. Clarity of thinking and wording of answers

You express your thoughts quite clearly. Sometimes you take time to think, and in my opinion this is very good. I did not notice any filler words, and you almost do not say "uh". Another plus is that you speak correctly and use words according to their meaning, without overusing slang and borrowed words. I hope that the coming years in IT will not spoil the purity and clarity of your speech.

### 5. Theoretical knowledge

This is your growth area. You still have things to study in project management, and it is good that you understand this yourself. You do not have to point it out in interviews, but it is useful to have an honest view of your own knowledge for yourself. As I said during the meeting, it is worth reading selected articles about certain processes or tasks in project management and immediately comparing them with the experience you have now. Where "something does not match", ask other colleagues or mentors.

### 6. Knowledge of tools and methods

Right now your level of using tools is limited. I advise you to experiment, try, look and compare. Do not take on complex tasks, use the "small steps" method. Here you learned to make one report, there you created a diagram or a flowchart, then you tried the same in another app, and in a third one, made conclusions, and move on...

### 7. Learning and self-development

Your energy is directed at learning and development, and this is your treasure! It is a very powerful side of your personality, and I sincerely wish you to use it to the maximum. You can learn and grow both in breadth and in depth. You are flexible and open enough to try different things, integrate them and get a unique expertise, which will be in high demand in the near future, when all the simple tasks can be solved with the help of AI

### 8. Finding yourself and setting goals

This point comes from the previous one. I am not sure that your future career is connected with project management. Not because you will not succeed (in fact, you have good potential), but because you are open to new things and ready to look at everything without prejudice and narrow views. I would advise you to choose one main direction for now and give it 70%-80% of your attention, and to direct the rest of your time and resources to experiments and exploring alternatives.
