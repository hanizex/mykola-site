About the resume: you need to rewrite it, because it gives almost no information and raises more questions than it answers.

If a recruiter runs the interview, it is not a technical interview. A recruiter cannot judge how relevant your experience is. They look at soft skills, and they can only understand on the surface how well you know the topic (they ask the questions that remain or appear after reading the resume).
