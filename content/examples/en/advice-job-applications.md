Hi.

As far as I know, the chance to get an answer when you apply through job sites is very small. Something like 1 in 20.

So, to have better results, I recommend not only applying but also finding the recruiters of these companies on LinkedIn. Write them a personal message that you found the vacancy and you are interested in it. You can also look for project managers of these companies and write to them.

This will raise your chance to get an answer and an interview invitation several times.
