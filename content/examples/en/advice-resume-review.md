I have a few remarks about your resume.

**Cons**

1. The font is very small and hard to read, because it is grey, not black.
2. You need to proofread it and fix the style and grammar mistakes. There are many of them.
3. It is not clear which role you are applying for: designer, analyst or manager. Focus on one role, and mention the additional background in the description of your work and results.
4. It does not say which languages you speak and at what level (this is important for international or foreign companies).

**Pros**

1. The measurable results are well written. I enjoy looking at them.
2. The structure of the resume is simple and short. There is no extra information, everything is to the point. My first resumes were much worse 😅
