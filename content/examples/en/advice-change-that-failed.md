From the very beginning, things went "wrong" here.

**1. People must be told about a change if it is expected from them**

The management had to tell the team officially, in writing, that changes were expected from them (releasing on time).

**2. Stakeholders had to be assigned using RACI**

Who does the work, who is responsible, who provides information, and who gets reports. Also officially, in writing.

The Accountable role in this process had to be yours.

**3. Goals had to be set clearly (with OKR or SMART)**

This is your task: to break the process into parts and to commit to the result together with the people who do the work.

But without points 1 and 2 this is impossible in a system that resists change.

**What can be fixed?**

Do points 1 and 2 and try again.
